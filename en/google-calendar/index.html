Google Calendar

Appointments straight into your Google Calendar.

Sofía never books over anything: she checks your busy times before offering a time and only confirms once the event exists.

How she books during a call

  1. Asks which service and which day.
  2. Checks that day's busy times in your Google Calendar.
  3. Offers two open times within your hours, with your minimum notice and buffer, using the service length.
  4. Takes the name and confirms the phone (offers the number they're calling from).
  5. Creates the event. She only says "confirmed" if the calendar accepted it; if the time got taken, she offers two others.
  6. If anything fails, she takes the request as a message and you confirm.

That's what happened in our real demo call: she offered Thursday 3:00 and 4:00 p.m., the caller chose 4:00 and the event was created. Listen

How it connects

  1. On a computer, open Google Calendar → Settings and pick your calendar.
  2. Under "Share with specific people", add hola@usecontesta.com with "Make changes to events".
  3. In your Contesta account, enter the calendar email and tap Check: we really test reading and writing.

With that permission, Contesta can see that calendar's events and create appointments. We use busy times to avoid double-booking. You can remove access any time; Sofía goes back to taking appointments as messages.

What goes in the event

Title: service and caller name. Description: "Agendada por Contesta", name, phone, service and, when it applies, the service address and the answers to your booking questions (for example, year, make and model).

Today's limits

  • One calendar: one appointment at a time. If you have several bays, chairs or crews, Sofía won't book two appointments at the same time.
  • Google Calendar only. It doesn't connect to other booking, shop-management or point-of-sale systems.
  • Books in Eastern time (Florida).
  • It doesn't take deposits or payments.
