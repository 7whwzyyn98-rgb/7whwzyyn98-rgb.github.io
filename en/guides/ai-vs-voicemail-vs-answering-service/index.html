Guide

AI receptionist, voicemail or answering service.

No option is best for everyone. Here's an honest comparison, including when Contesta isn't the right fit.

OptionCostWhat it solvesBest fit
VoicemailFree or included in your plan.Many callers hang up without leaving a message; you call back later.Few calls and customers who don't need an immediate answer.
Human answering serviceVaries widely by provider and volume.A person answers, takes messages and sometimes books. May have limited hours or per-minute pricing.Sensitive calls that need human judgment.
AI receptionist (Contesta)From $197 a month with 150 minutes.Answers instantly, 24/7, in Spanish or English; books in Google Calendar and emails you the summary. Only knows what you teach her.Appointment businesses that miss calls while working or after hours.

When Contesta isn't the right fit

  • If you barely get calls, voicemail is probably enough.
  • If your calls need human judgment (complex complaints, sensitive cases), a person is better.
  • If you book in a system other than Google Calendar, or need several appointments at the same time, it doesn't fit today.

How to decide

Count how many calls you miss in a normal week and how many of those were for an appointment. If it's several, listen to the demo call and try the live line: (813) 722-1120.
