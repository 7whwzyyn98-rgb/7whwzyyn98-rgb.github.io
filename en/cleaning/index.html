Who it's for

A receptionist for cleaning services.

You're cleaning a house and the phone won't stop. Sofía answers, asks for the address, checks it's in your area and books the cleaning or estimate in your calendar.

What she does for you

  • Asks for the service address (street, city and ZIP) and reads it back.
  • Checks it's inside the area you set; if not, she takes a message instead of booking.
  • Asks your questions before booking, for example how many bedrooms and bathrooms or whether there are pets, and saves the answers in the appointment.
  • Books cleanings or estimates in your Google Calendar with each service's length.
  • Gives your "from" prices and explains what each service includes, based on what you wrote.
Example setup (illustrative)

Standard clean (1-2 bedrooms) — $120 — 2 h Deep clean — from $220 — 4 h In-home estimate — free — 30 min Area: Tampa, Brandon and Riverview Booking questions: bedrooms, bathrooms and pets

You write this in your account, with your real prices. Sofía only quotes what's there.

Before you sign up, know this

  • One appointment at a time on one calendar: if you have several crews, Sofía won't book two cleanings at the same time.
  • She doesn't calculate custom prices: she gives your "from" prices and takes the details for your quote.
  • She doesn't take deposits.
