Guide

What a bilingual AI receptionist can and can't do.

What Sofía does today, what she doesn't, and an example from a real call.

What she does well

  • Answers in Spanish or English, and switches if the caller switches.
  • Answers the usual questions from the information you approved: hours, prices, address.
  • Books into your Google Calendar without overlapping busy times, and only confirms once the event exists.
  • Takes messages with name, number and reason, and emails you the summary.
  • Transfers urgent calls to your cell during business hours (Pro and Max).
  • Recognizes the caller's number and offers it as the callback number.

What she doesn't do (yet)

  • It doesn't invent prices, timelines or promises: if it isn't in your information, she takes a message.
  • It doesn't send text messages yet.
  • It doesn't take payments or deposits.
  • It doesn't connect to booking systems other than Google Calendar, or book two appointments at the same time.
  • It doesn't give medical, legal or financial advice.
  • On very noisy lines she may ask the caller to repeat; she never guesses names or numbers.
