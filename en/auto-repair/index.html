Who it's for

A receptionist for auto repair and detailing shops.

You're under a car or covered in grease. Sofía answers, books the service in your Google Calendar and notes year, make and model.

What she does for you

  • Books oil changes, diagnostics, alignments or washes with each service's length.
  • Asks year, make and model (or whatever you set as booking questions) and saves it in the appointment.
  • Gives your "from" prices and hours, and explains how to get there.
  • If someone wants a repair quote, she takes the details for you to call back: she doesn't invent prices.
  • After hours, she takes a message or books the next open time, as you prefer.
Example setup (illustrative)

Oil change — $45 — 30 min Diagnostics — $80 — 60 min Interior detail — from $120 — 2 h Booking questions: year, make and model of the car

You write this in your account, with your real prices. Sofía only quotes what's there.

Before you sign up, know this

  • One appointment at a time: if you have several bays, Sofía won't book two cars at the same time on the same calendar.
  • She doesn't check parts inventory or the status of a car in the shop.
  • She doesn't connect to shop-management software; only Google Calendar.
