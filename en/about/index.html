About

An honest receptionist, made in Tampa.

Contesta is a small Tampa company. We build a Spanish-first AI receptionist for local businesses that lose customers when they can't pick up.

How we work

  • Sofía always says she's a virtual assistant and that the call may be recorded.
  • She only answers with information you approved. If she doesn't know something, she takes a message.

  • We tell you what she doesn't do yet, before you sign up.
