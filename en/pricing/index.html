Billing questions

When am I charged?

Nothing during the 14-day trial. A card is added to start the trial, and the monthly price of the plan you chose starts when the trial ends, unless you cancel first.

Can I add my card today?

Not yet: we're enabling secure online payment. You can create your account and finish setting up your receptionist; we'll email you when you can add the card and activate.

What does the $297 setup cover?

Building your receptionist and your Contesta number. The first 5 businesses don't pay it.

Can I change plans?

Yes. Before adding your card you change it in your account; after that, email hola@usecontesta.com.

How do I cancel?

Email hola@usecontesta.com. There's no contract. To send calls back to your phone right away, turn forwarding off from your phone.
